QIHUY CITY

An open-world crime story · Sunset Coast

Riko Vale is back in Qihuy City — nine roads of sun, sea and bad decisions, across eleven districts: the glass towers downtown, the quiet houses of the suburb, the boardwalk with its ferris wheel, the container port, colourful Old Town, the marina, Market Row, the ferry terminal and its lighthouse, the casino strip, and the airport with its runway and hangars. The fixer Mara has twenty-four jobs for him: steal the wheels, run the package, lift the harbor cash, and twenty-one more — fares, raids, a hospital run, a taxi shift, an ambulance run, a port delivery, a neon run through two districts, a five-star airport heist on a motorbike, a casino-strip job, a ferry-terminal run, a market hustle, a sunset finale, and one last getaway with two helicopters overhead — and then a choice: Mara's garage or Dara's casino, with three more jobs on the side he picks. Between jobs, tune the car at the parts shop, walk into the corner store, the Golden Palm and the safehouse, and mind the traffic — rush hour is thick, roadworks narrow the lanes, and pedestrians dart across the road. Or skip the story: a vigilante shift or a taxi career runs straight from this screen. Steal cars, dodge the patrols, mind the weather — rain makes the roads slick and fog blinds the cops — and don't let the wanted stars catch up with you.
